## 2. DRILLING / PIERCING CAPABILITY

| Material | Thickness Min | Thickness Max | Angle | Size | Type | Pierce Size | Lance | Hole Size | Machinability |
|----------|---------------|---------------|-------|------|------|-------------|-------|-----------|---------------|
| SS | Min | 3.2 | Outer angle ≤90° | 0.5 | Step | 6 | 10 | > 1/2 thickness | OK |
| SS | 16 | 16 | - | No | Step | 10 | 10 | > thickness | OK |
| SUS, etc. | Min | 5 | Outer angle ≤90° | 0.5 | Step | 6 | 10 | > 1/2 thickness | OK |
| SUS, etc. | 15 | 16 | - | No | Step | 10 | 10 | Φ10 or more | OK |
| SUS, etc. | 20 | Max | - | No | Taper | 10 | 10 | > thickness | OK |
| Aluminum | Min | 0.5 | Outer angle ≤90° | 0.5 | Step | 6 | 10 | > thickness × 0.8 | OK |
| Aluminum | 16 | Max | - | No | Step | 10 | 10 | > thickness | OK |
| Copper/Brass | Min | 5 | Outer angle ≤90° | 0.5 | Step | 6 | 10 | > 1/2 thickness | OK |
| *Cannot scribe | 6 | Max | Outer angle ≤90° | 0.5 | Step | 6 | 10 | > thickness | OK |

**Key columns:**
- **Type**: Step = stepped lance, Taper = tapered lance
- **Pierce Size**: Minimum punch diameter
- **Lance**: Distance between pierce points
- **Hole Size**: Minimum achievable hole diameter relative to material thickness
