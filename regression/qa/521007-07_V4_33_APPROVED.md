# 521007-07 — V4.33 holistic challenge / reference-exposure regression

**Status:** USER APPROVED / PUSH 2026-10-07.

## FACT

The first E2E run produced a structurally readable review DXF but failed upstream interpretation/completeness in several different ways:

- some bent/multi-face codes were downgraded to VIEWS_ONLY before the solver had demonstrated complete source exhaustion and a specific remaining degree of freedom;
- one part used a shared bend-edge length as if it were the whole blank extent and omitted source feature members;
- two constituent codes in a welded drawing were not separated by a proved part/view/DATA ownership graph and were represented from essentially the same unresolved source bundle;
- a PDF-only part was reduced to a small source-view subset even though independently proved drawing geometry remained executable;
- after a sample DXF was supplied, the interpretation improved, creating a risk of hindsight: knowledge learned from the sample could be restated as if it had been established from the original source.

The operator additionally confirmed that FLAG/Magenta geometry in the sample represents fields still being confirmed with the customer, not approved final geometry.

## ROOT_CAUSE

Primary:

`AI_INTERPRETATION_GAP + SOURCE_LEDGER_PLAUSIBILITY_GAP + PREMATURE_VIEWS_ONLY`

Secondary:

`PART_IDENTITY_GAP + REFERENCE_EXPOSURE_HINDSIGHT_RISK + REGRESSION_COVERAGE_GAP`.

This is not primarily a DXF serializer defect. Canonical-member equality and saved-DXF entity identity can both pass when the source ledger itself is incomplete or wrongly scoped.

## APPROVED LESSON

1. Run a holistic engineering challenge after the first source-only solve and before canonicalization.
2. Treat suspicious source-to-ledger usage, unexplained unused views, zero proved members against substantial physical DATA, part-identity collisions and bend-edge/blank-extent semantic reuse as mandatory re-solve triggers.
3. Before VIEWS_ONLY, prove source exhaustion, run the challenge, record the actual unresolved degree of freedom, and preserve every independently proved source-view member in the review DXF.
4. Local uncertainty remains local; it does not erase an independently proved flat.
5. Freeze a source-only snapshot before exposing an audit/calibration sample. Post-reference understanding must retain REFERENCE_ASSISTED provenance unless new non-reference evidence is recorded.
6. Sample FLAG/Magenta geometry remains CUSTOMER_CONFIRMATION_PENDING and cannot close the source automatically.

## REGRESSION CASE

Executable:
- `regression/tools/check_521007_07_v433.py`
- `tests/test_v433_holistic_reference_guards.py`

The regression uses semantic/synthetic fixtures to test the demonstrated mechanisms. It does not encode sample coordinates as construction rules.

Case intent:

- a source with physical members but zero proved ledger members must trigger `SOURCE_MEMBER_CENSUS_GAP`;
- VIEWS_ONLY before source exhaustion/challenge or with missing proved source-view members must fail;
- a shared bend-edge dimension reused as blank extent without same-physical-parameter proof must fail;
- two independent codes assigned the same complete source bundle must trigger `PART_IDENTITY_COLLISION`;
- an unresolved pre-reference claim cannot become source-PROVED after reference exposure when its only new provenance is reference/sample;
- sample-FLAG geometry cannot be promoted to PROVED;
- a local unresolved feature cannot force VIEWS_ONLY when a defensible flat independently exists.

## BLIND CALIBRATION CONTRACT

For a real future blind replay of this job, the solver must receive only the original PDF + customer DATA through source-only freeze. The sample DXF is withheld until comparison. A run executed after the model has already seen the sample is not evidence of source-only solvability.

## NON-GENERALIZATION

Do not hardcode this job's part dimensions, attachment direction, constituent numbering, radii or sample coordinates into future construction. The approved rule is the challenge/provenance mechanism, source-view completeness fallback and identity/semantic hard gates.
