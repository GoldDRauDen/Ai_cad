## A. FACT → ROOT_CAUSE

### 056940 — control
Direct physical comparison was MATCH. V4.19 must not mutate already proved flat-only geometry merely because the new multi-face solver exists.

### 056942 — same total, wrong face order
The old AI obtained the correct flat envelope `100 × 168.8` from `150 + 23.2 - 4.4`, but placed the double BEND at local `18.8 / 23.2`. The approved sample/source mapping puts the 150 face first, therefore the correct pair is `145.6 / 150.0`. The two Ø7 features belong to the small/free-edge flange.

**Root cause:** overall flat arithmetic was treated as proof of face order. It is not. Swapping the two OD faces leaves the total unchanged while moving BEND and feature transforms.

### 056956 — multi-axis/local flange topology
Source/sample close `106 + 222 + 106 - 2×3.8 = 426.4` and the orthogonal extent `90 + 29 - 3.8 = 115.2`. The ordered X chain is `106 | 222 | 106`, giving X bend pairs `102.2/106.0` and `320.4/324.2`. The upper local flange family uses Y pair `86.2/90.0`, but those BEND lines exist only on the material segments that actually carry the local flanges/reliefs. The 4×Ø5.5 and 4×Ø6.5 families belong to different proved faces.

**Root cause:** the old AI closed the bbox and feature family counts but stopped at VIEWS_ONLY because it did not build/solve explicit face adjacency + local bend domains.

### 057042 — signed longitudinal chain plus local transverse channel
The approved longitudinal sequence is `39.8 | 128.8 | 21.3` with Nobi `4.62`, producing flat length `180.66` and X pairs `35.18/39.80`, `159.36/163.98`. The two longitudinal bends have opposite fold signs (Z/S behavior). Independently, the transverse chain `15 | 35 | 15` gives `55.76` and Y pairs `10.38/15.00`, `40.76/45.38`, only across the source-proved local channel region rather than the full blank. Feature families include 2×Ø5.5, 2×Ø4.5 and 2×2-M4 laser pilot holes; feature ownership must be retained through local-face transforms.

**Root cause:** the old AI recognized dimensions/features but lacked a signed multi-axis face graph, so it could not prove topology/domain even though the source views plus isometric constrained the formed part.

## B. PROPOSED LESSON → APPROVED PRODUCTION HARDENING

V4.18 already contained face-first reconstruction and non-commutative face order. The new nonduplicate lesson is **enforceable intermediate state and validation**, not another prose reminder.

For every bent/multi-face part, production now requires:
1. mandatory FACE_GRAPH records;
2. dimension witness + semantic ownership;
3. feature host-face/local-datum ownership;
4. ordered face chain;
5. signed bend orientation/face-normal state;
6. actual material-domain BEND ownership;
7. constraint-closure/ambiguity gate;
8. fold-back + source reprojection validation before serialization;
9. normal DXF read-back afterward.

Full contract: `references/FACE_GRAPH_UNFOLD_PROTOCOL.md`.

## C. Negative assertions

FAIL if:
- total flat length is used to choose between swapped face orders;
- a feature is placed directly in global flat coordinates before host-face/local-datum proof;
- unsigned 90° bends are allowed to erase U-vs-Z/S orientation;
- a double BEND line crosses empty space, relief or an unrelated local region;
- bbox/feature-count closure is accepted while a competing face graph remains equally valid;
- a flat is marked production PASS without fold-back/reprojection satisfying the source-proved formed constraints;
- sample DXF coordinates are reverse-fit into NEW_PDF_TO_DXF construction;
- V4.19 changes already matching 056940 merely to satisfy the new mechanism.
