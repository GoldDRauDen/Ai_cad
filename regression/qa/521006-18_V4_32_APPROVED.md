# 521006-18 — V4.32 bend attachment / formed-profile OD regression

**Status:** USER APPROVED / PUSH 2026-10-07.

## FACT

Reference audit exposed independent mechanisms that V4.31 did not hard-block:

- `058648`: AI attached the flange by the graphical edge nearest the main view. The physical bend connection was the opposite edge, proved by the formed/profile relationship. This reversed `BEND_EDGE <-> FREE_EDGE` and displaced flange-owned features after unfold.
- `058653` and `058655`: the profile view is a **measurement/reference view used to obtain the actual bent outside lengths**. It is not CUT geometry. AI used apparent face dimensions directly instead of constructing/measuring the correct outside contributions before Nobi.
- `058649` / `058650`: an R3 edge-open notch with overall depth 8 was interpreted as center setback 8, producing depth 11. The dimension witness controls deepest physical depth, not the arc center.
- `058652`: whole-part 180-degree representation remained physically equivalent and must not be rejected merely for paper orientation.
- `058654`: customer confirmation remains pending; the sample cannot close that unresolved source field.

## ROOT_CAUSE

Primary:

`AI_TOPOLOGY_ERROR + BEND_ATTACHMENT_EDGE_OWNERSHIP_GAP`

Secondary:

`FORMED_OD_OWNERSHIP_ERROR + DIMENSION_ENDPOINT_SEMANTICS_ERROR + REGRESSION_COVERAGE_GAP`

This is not a serializer defect. Canonical -> saved-DXF identity may pass while the canonical model itself carries the wrong physical attachment or OD semantics.

## APPROVED LESSON

For every bend, production must prove:

`FACE_A.edge_i <-> BEND <-> FACE_B.edge_j`

before unfold. `NEAREST_VIEW_EDGE` and paper proximity are forbidden evidence.

Thickness/hidden-line evidence is optional. Formed/profile/side/section topology, projection correspondence, witness connectivity and other explicit source cues may prove the attachment.

When actual outside length is required, reconstruct the formed profile and measure/derive `OD_actual` with explicit thickness/offset ownership, then apply Nobi. A profile/reference measurement view is not automatically manufacturing CUT geometry.

Feature datums on bent faces must remain bound to physical edge identity (`BEND_EDGE`, `FREE_EDGE`, etc.) through the local-face -> flat transform.
