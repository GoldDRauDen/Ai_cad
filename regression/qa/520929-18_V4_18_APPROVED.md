## C. 056905 — BOXMET signed orientation and BEND trimming

The old AI matched the `816.72 × 580.72` outer flat envelope but mirrored the four BOXMET slit directions across the local bend-side relation and trimmed dashed BEND by corner proximity.

For local base frame `+X` right, `+Y` up, `L >= W`:
- BL `B=(t,0)`: short ray `(0,+)`, lead `(-,-)`
- BR `B=(L-t,0)`: short ray `(0,+)`, lead `(+,-)`
- TR `B=(L-t,W)`: short ray `(0,-)`, lead `(+,+)`

V4.16 parameters remain unchanged: `SlitLen=t+0.2`, applicable `LeadVal`, tangent `R0.5`, one connected Green `LINE + ARC + LINE` per slit.

BEND rule: construct slit CUT first and evaluate actual geometric intersection. Split/trim only for real interior crossing/overlap; empty intersection keeps BEND continuous; endpoint-only contact does not create an artificial gap. Do not trim from corner/retreat-point proximity.

FAIL if a slit is locally mirrored by appearance or if BEND is split without actual intersection.
