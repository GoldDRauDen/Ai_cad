## PROPOSED_LESSON — USER APPROVED

### SOURCE_MANUFACTURING_NOTE_CENSUS

Every production-affecting printed/handwritten/revision instruction must be accounted before an affected feature family may be `NO`.

For a custom source-instructed `スリット/SLIT`:
1. create the custom slit only when source instruction proves it;
2. use source-specific size/value, quantity, host, position and direction;
3. do not auto-add a slit from BEND existence or mechanical intuition;
4. do not assume bend-center placement;
5. when an explicit slit value controls the feature, do not replace it with `t+0.2`, `t*sin(theta)`, Nobi, or a reference-derived value;
6. when identity is proved but construction fields remain unresolved, keep the family routed and FLAG those fields instead of deleting the feature.

Existing crossing-bend, opposite-fold `R=t`, BOXMET and other separately-triggered relief mechanisms remain independent.
