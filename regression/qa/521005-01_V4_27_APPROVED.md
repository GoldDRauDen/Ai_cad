## APPROVED V4.27 lesson

1. `REVERSED_E_ABOVE_BARCODE` is scoped to the immediately associated barcode/code/detail cluster and records `WELD_REQUIRED_CUE`.
2. The cue proves a welding-process expectation, not KEGAKI geometry.
3. If a welded RIB/gusset/support child cannot self-locate, KEGAKI locating on the main part must be `YES` when proved or at least `UNKNOWN` until locating/contact evidence is exhausted; it must not silently disappear from census/output.
4. A specific KEGAKI instruction independently requires KEGAKI even without the barcode cue.
5. If neither the reversed-e cue nor a specific KEGAKI instruction exists for that cluster, do not infer welding or create KEGAKI solely from the presence of a secondary RIB/support.
6. Never copy the full child contour as KEGAKI without proved locating/contact function.
7. Existing slot semantics and datum/witness rules remain authoritative; this release adds regression/guardrail coverage rather than duplicate formulas.
