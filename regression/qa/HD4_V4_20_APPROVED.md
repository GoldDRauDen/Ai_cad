Approved lessons:
1. Green is not synonymous with PIASU. Green POINT + applicable PIASU semantics is PIASU; connected Green LINE/ARC can be slit/relief.
2. KEGAKI is independent Red locating geometry and is audited separately.
3. PL2 has locating KEGAKI; PL1 is edge-located and does not receive a copied equivalent mark. Do not invent symmetric marks.
4. RIB1/RIB2 KEGAKI represents main-part contact/position footprint after forming, not a blind copy of the complete RIB contour; it may split across formed faces/material regions.
5. RIB part and main-part RIB KEGAKI are independent cancellation/output decisions.
6. 90°/150° slit depth behavior is distinct from Nobi.

Job-specific geometry is evidence, not reusable coordinates for unrelated parts.
